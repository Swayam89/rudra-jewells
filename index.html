01 Our Approach

From your idea to your hand.

  1. Stage 1

    Your idea

    Tell us what you have in mind: a sketch, a photograph, a family piece, or simply a feeling.

  2. Stage 2

    The design

    We draw it with you, refining the proportions until the piece is unmistakably yours.

  3. Stage 3

    The setting

    The metal is formed and the setting is raised to hold the stone.

  4. Stage 4

    The stone

    The stone is chosen with you, then seated in its setting.

  5. Stage 5

    Yours

    Finished, polished and placed in your hands. Curated just for you.
