About us

Your Ideation, Our Craftsmanship

For more than 25 years, Rudra Jewells has turned the ideas our clients bring us into jewellery made for them alone.

You bring the thought: a sketch, a photograph, a family stone, a memory or simply a feeling. We sit with you, shape it into a design, and our craftsmen give it form, paying attention to every setting, every finish and every detail.

Every piece is personalised, so no two are alike. It is jewellery that is not picked off a shelf, but curated just for you.
